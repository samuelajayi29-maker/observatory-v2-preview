Known gaps

This series is a feed, not yet a register

Jobs signals are recorded and published as they arrive, but the structured fields above are not yet being extracted, so there is no row-level dataset to download and no country totals. Publishing a register before the extraction exists would mean estimating numbers we cannot source, which is the thing this record exists to avoid.

What is being built

An extraction step that reads each recorded signal and writes country, employer, headcount and wage with its source, so the Jobs series carries the same row-level provenance as Infrastructure and Access.
