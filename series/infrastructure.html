How this record is kept

What counts as a project

A facility enters the register when a named sponsor makes a public commitment with a capacity figure attached. Colocation resold from another operator's floor is excluded, so the same megawatt is never counted twice. Portfolio capital raises are their own rows and never added to project capacity.

What counts as energised

A facility is energised when it serves customer load, evidenced by an operator statement naming the date. Announcements, permits and ribbon-cuttings do not count. Digital Realty's Nairobi Two, opened 7 September 2026, is the only row in this register that qualifies.

Known gaps

16 of 22 rows carry no published megawatt figure, including the $1bn Egyptian facility and the Kenyan projects reported in shillings. Those rows appear with capacity marked unknown and are excluded from the chart above. Published continental estimates put Africa at 360 MW active and 238 MW under construction, so this register covers a fraction of the market, and says so.

Revisions

When a field changes, the change is logged with its date and source and the previous value is kept. Nothing is silently overwritten.
